# Chapter 13: The Illusion of the World

![](images/image-1.jpg)

(167)
Don’t follow the crowd.
Don’t live on autopilot.
Don’t cling to wrong beliefs.
And don’t get stuck in the cycle of worldly chasing.

(168)
Wake up.
Don’t drift.
Live right.
Those who walk the path—
they’re happy now and later.

(169)
Stay upright.
Don’t take the low road.
It’s not just about reward—
it’s about real peace, now and after.

(170)
See the world for what it is:
a bubble, a mirage.
If you can really see that,
even Death can’t catch you.

(171)
Look at this world—
decked out like a royal parade.
But it’s a trap.
Fools get caught.
The wise let it pass by.

(172)
The one who was lost,
but wakes up—
they shine like the moon
breaking through the clouds.

(173)
The one who turns their wrongs into rights—
through real, good action—
also shines
like the full moon after a storm.

(174)
This world is mostly blind.
Only a few truly see.
Only a few break free—
like birds slipping the net.

(175)
Swans follow the sun’s path.
The wise follow the way of truth,
flying above the world,
leaving Mara behind.

(176)
A liar,
who scorns truth and mocks the afterlife—
there’s no evil they won’t sink into.

(177)
Greedy people don’t reach the higher realms.
And fools don’t see the value of giving.
But the wise?
They rejoice in generosity—
and they rise by it.

(178)
Better than ruling the world.
Better than heaven.
Better than cosmic power—
is the first taste of enlightenment:
Stream Entry.
