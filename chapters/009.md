# Chapter 9: Drop by Drop

![](images/image-3.jpg)

(116)
Move fast toward the good.
Don’t let your mind drift into harm.
The slower you are to act kindly,
the easier it is for darkness to creep in.

(117)
If you’ve done something wrong,
don’t do it again.
Don’t tell yourself it’s no big deal.
Evil stacks up,
and the weight gets heavier.

(118)
If you’ve done something right,
do it again.
Let yourself feel good about it.
Kindness adds up too—
and it brings real joy.

(119)
The wrongdoer might think,
"Hey, everything's fine."
But when the consequences hit—
the pain is sharp and real.

(120)
The good person might struggle at first.
But when their actions finally bear fruit—
the joy is deep and lasting.

(121)
Don’t brush off small bad choices,
saying, “This won’t affect me.”
Even a giant jar fills up drop by drop.
So does a life—
with every careless action.

(122)
And don’t dismiss the good, either.
Even tiny kindnesses matter.
Drop by drop,
they fill your life with light.

(123)
Avoid evil like a trader avoids a dangerous road
when he’s carrying treasure and has no bodyguards.
Avoid it like poison—because it is.

(124)
If your hand has no wound,
you can carry poison—it won’t hurt you.
In the same way,
evil can’t touch the person who’s done no wrong.

(125)
But if you hurt someone kind and blameless,
the harm you meant for them
comes flying back—
like dust blown into the wind.

(126)
Some are born into families,
some into pain.
Some rise to joy,
and some go beyond it all.

(127)
You can’t outrun your actions.
Not to the skies,
not to the seas,
not into the caves of the mountains.

(128)
And you can’t outrun death.
It finds everyone—
wherever they hide.
