# Chapter 5: The Fool’s Journey

![](images/image-5.jpg)

(60)
The night feels endless when you can’t sleep.
The road feels endless when your body’s tired.
Life feels endless—when you live without wisdom.

(61)
If you can’t find someone wiser than you to walk with,
walk alone.
Better to be solo than dragged down by fools.

(62)
The fool worries:
“I have kids. I have money.”
But even your self isn’t really yours.
So where are these kids?
Where is this wealth?

(63)
A fool who knows he’s a fool—
that’s a start.
But a fool who thinks he’s wise?
That’s the real fool.

(64)
You can hang out with wise people your whole life—
but if you don’t listen or change,
you’re just a spoon in a soup:
close to the flavor,
but never tasting it.

(65)
On the flip side,
even a short talk with someone wise
can open your heart—
like a tongue tasting flavor instantly.

(66)
Fools hurt themselves
with the dumb things they do.
Their bad actions follow them
like sour fruit.

(67)
Bad choices feel good at first—
but later, they sting.
You cry.
You regret.

(68)
Good choices?
No regrets.
Only peace, and deep happiness.

(69)
Until the damage shows up,
the fool thinks:
“This is sweet! Like honey!”
But when the consequences hit—
the grief is real.

(70)
You could starve yourself for months,
trying to look holy—
but if you haven’t understood the truth,
you’re not even worth a sixteenth
of one person who has.

(71)
Evil doesn’t always show up fast.
It’s like milk—it sours slowly.
But eventually it curdles.
And when it does,
the fool burns—like fire hiding under ash.

(72)
Knowledge in a fool’s hands?
It backfires.
It cuts deeper.
It wrecks what goodness he had.

(73)
The fool wants fame.
He wants to be “top monk,”
the voice everyone listens to,
the name everyone praises.

(74)
He thinks,
“Let everyone follow me.
Let them say, ‘He did it.’
Let my name be in every story.”
And with that…
his pride and craving just keep growing.

(75)
There are two paths:
the path of ego, and the path of awakening.
If you know the difference—
don’t get lost in applause.
Choose detachment.
Choose peace.
