# Chapter 17: Cool the Fire Within

![](images/image-5.jpg)

(221)
Drop your anger.
Let go of your ego.
Cut the chains.
If you're not clinging to your thoughts or your body—
you won’t suffer.

(222)
The one who reins in their anger
like a charioteer stopping a wild horse—
that’s a real master.
Anyone can hold the reins.
Few can steer the storm.

(223)
Don’t fight fire with fire.
Overcome anger with calm.
Beat cruelty with kindness.
Drown greed with generosity.
Defeat lies with truth.

(224)
Speak honestly.
Stay cool.
Give what you can, even if it's little.
Do these,
and you’ll walk with the divine.

(225)
Those who live gently—
who do no harm and stay steady—
they reach the deathless place.
And there, grief no longer follows.

(226)
The ones who stay mindful
day and night,
with their hearts fixed on freedom—
they clean away their inner darkness.

(227)
Hey Atula—
this has always been true:
People will judge you if you say nothing,
judge you if you say too much,
and judge you if you're in the middle.
There’s no escape from criticism.

(228)
There’s never been, and never will be,
someone who’s only praised
or only blamed.

(229)
But the person who earns the respect
of the wise—day after day—
that one is pure gold.
Solid. Quiet. True.

(230)
Who could blame someone like that?
Even the gods admire him.
Even Brahma nods in respect.

(231)
Keep your body in check.
Let your actions be clean.
Drop harmful habits.
Live with integrity.

(232)
Speak with kindness.
Drop the sharp tone.
Control what leaves your mouth.

(233)
Even your thoughts—
keep them clear,
gentle,
honest.

(234)
The truly wise
are in control—
body, speech, and mind.
That’s real strength.
