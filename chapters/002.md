# Chapter 2: Stay Awake

![](images/image-2.jpg)

(21)
Staying mindful is the way to true freedom.
Living on autopilot? That’s a slow death.
The aware never really die.
But the unaware—
they’re already gone, just walking shadows.

(22)
The wise get this.
They know mindfulness is gold.
They celebrate staying awake.
They live where the Noble Ones live—
in clarity.

(23)
Those who stay focused,
who meditate and keep showing up,
who don't quit—
they taste something beyond this world.
They touch freedom itself.

(24)
People like that?
They glow.
They live clean, they live sharp.
They’re calm, clear, grounded, strong.
Their lives shine brighter and brighter.

(25)
So build your island.
Use effort, discipline, wisdom.
Make it solid.
Let no storm in this life drown you.

(26)
The clueless chase distractions.
But the wise?
They guard their mindfulness
like it’s their most prized possession.

(27)
Don’t drift off.
Don’t get caught in chasing pleasure.
Only those who stay present,
and stay grounded,
find lasting happiness.

(28)
It’s like standing on a mountaintop—
when you rise above distraction,
you see the world clearly:
the crowd still stuck in the chaos,
grasping, hurting, spinning.

(29)
The mindful one stands out.
While others sleepwalk through life,
he moves like a racehorse
leaving tired ponies in the dust.

(30)
Even Indra, king of the gods,
earned his place by being alert.
Mindfulness is always respected.
Neglect is always regretted.

(31)
A monk who loves mindfulness,
who treats distraction like danger,
moves forward like a wildfire—
burning through every chain,
big or small.

(32)
That same monk?
He won’t fall.
He’s walking right into Nibbana.
