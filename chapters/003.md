# Chapter 3: Train the Wild Mind

![](images/image-3.jpg)

(33)
Just like a bowmaker bends the arrow straight,
the wise shape their minds—
even when it’s jumpy, messy, and hard to manage.

(34)
The mind out of water is like a fish on land—
flopping, gasping, thrashing.
That's what it’s like when we’re stuck in the world’s illusions.
Get out before Mara reels you back in.

(35)
It’s wild, this mind—
fast, greedy, chasing what it craves.
But if you can tame it, even a little…
you’ll find real joy.

(36)
So protect your mind.
It's subtle, sneaky, and wants what it wants.
But if you guard it well,
it becomes a source of peace, not pain.

(37)
The mind lives deep inside—
formless, wandering, lonely.
But if you calm it, you break free
from all the chains of Mara.

(38)
You can’t grow wise
if your mind is shaky,
your faith unsure,
and you ignore what’s true.

(39)
But if your heart is clear,
not drowning in desire or hate—
you’ve got nothing to fear.
You’ve risen beyond karma itself.

(40)
Know your body’s fragile—like pottery.
So build your mind strong, like a fortress.
Arm yourself with wisdom.
Fight Mara.
And once you win, don’t cling to the victory. Just walk on.

(41)
One day—maybe soon—
this body will just lie there,
empty, still,
like a forgotten log.

(42)
No enemy can hurt you
as much as your own messy, untrained mind.

(43)
And no one—not even your parents—
can help you like your own mind can,
if you guide it well.
